## What the statuses mean

- **Resolved**: the reported live mechanism or current documentation claim is
  corrected within the admitted scope; relevant evidence is linked. Specific
  specimen/test limits are retained rather than silently promoted to proof.
- **Partly resolved**: there is a concrete improvement and a named remaining gap.
- **Open**: the reported mechanism or wording remains on the live path.
- **Needs evidence**: code changed, but independent serialization/specimen proof
  remains insufficient to settle the interpretation.
- **Accepted scope limit**: an explicitly agreed discovery, feature or resource
  boundary. This does not certify unsupported formats or behavior.

## Recommended correction order

1. Establish active membership for MXF Preface/ContentStorage and OMF HEAD before
   projecting owned media (F40/F14). Retaining objects alone does not establish
   that they are active members.
2. Preserve ambiguity and decisive-reference incompleteness through semantic
   projection, including contradictory PackageUID occurrences (F06/F07).
3. Qualify malformed audio-summary extensions and retain their separately decoded
   fields (F20/F21); replace the remaining UID-width codec gate with format/class
   evidence (F17).
4. Address the narrower remaining range, locator, slot/timecode and source-snapshot
   boundaries (F09/F19/F15/F16/F24), with specimen/source evidence where needed.
5. Correct the remaining automatic-rebuild UI wording (F32), and complete mapping
   provenance/private serialization evidence (F38/F18).

These are follow-up corrections, not changes secretly included in this pass.
The [metadata evidence-state report](metadata-evidence-states-2026-10-07.md)
describes the implemented bookkeeping and incomplete-descriptor PCM correction.
Unknown field coverage remains explicit; it does not make the open graph
ownership or audio-summary interpretations correct.
